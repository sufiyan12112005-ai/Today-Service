Common front-load washer problems

Machine is not spinningThe drum may fail to rotate at the expected speed or leave clothes very wet.
Water will not drainWater remains in the drum after the programme ends.
Door does not lock or openThe machine may refuse to start or keep the door locked.
Water leakage or unusual noiseLeaks, vibration or grinding sounds require inspection before deciding on a repair.
Error codes or machine not startingA fault code or power problem can have several causes depending on the brand and model.

What happens during a repair visit?

When you request service, share the brand, model if available, visible error code and symptoms. Our team first confirms availability in your Bengaluru locality. During a visit, the technician examines the machine and discusses any repair options and applicable costs before proceeding with approved work.

We do not promise that every part or model can be repaired. Availability and price depend on the diagnosis and parts.

Safe steps before booking

  • Note any error code and when it appears.
  • Check whether the water supply is open and whether the electricity is available.
  • If you notice burning smells, smoke or water near electrical connections, switch off power if safe and avoid operating the machine.
  • Do not open electrical panels or bypass door-lock safety mechanisms yourself.
