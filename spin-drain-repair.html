Common symptoms worth reporting

Clothes remain wetThe spin programme may not complete, or water may still be inside the appliance.
Water does not emptyThe washing machine leaves standing water in the drum or tub.
Machine stops before the spinThe programme pauses, ends early or displays an error code.
Strong vibration or loud soundsThe machine vibrates or makes new sounds when a cycle starts.

These symptoms do not identify a particular failed component. A technician must inspect the appliance before deciding whether any part needs attention.

What can you check safely?

  • Observe and note when the cycle stops and whether an error code appears.
  • Make sure the washing machine is not overloaded.
  • Check whether the outlet hose is obviously crushed or bent, without opening electrical components.
  • Stop operating the appliance if there is unsafe leakage, overheating, smoke or an electrical smell.

If the issue continues, avoid repeated cycles that could worsen the problem. Contact a technician.
