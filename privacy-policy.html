Privacy Policy for TodayService.in

This policy explains how information is handled when you use TodayService.in to request washing machine repair service in Bengaluru. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

1. Who operates this website

TodayService is an independent washing machine repair provider serving customers in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Our own technicians provide eligible front-load and top-load washing machine inspection and repair services.

For questions about this policy or your personal information, email todayservice942@gmail.com or call +91 9148104704.

2. What information we collect

When you submit a service request, the form collects the name, mobile number, Bengaluru PIN code, washing machine type and brand. You may also choose to provide a locality, issue description and additional notes. If you choose “Other” for your brand, the form collects the brand name you enter.

The website may retain advertising referral details included in a visit URL, such as UTM campaign parameters and Google advertising click identifiers. Basic technical data may also be processed by the hosting provider and the systems used to deliver the site.

Please do not provide payment card numbers, passwords, identification documents or other sensitive details through the booking form.

3. Why we use the information

  • To review your repair request, contact you and check service coverage.
  • To arrange inspection or repair by TodayService technicians when the details are agreed.
  • To keep records of enquiries and respond to customer questions or complaints.
  • To understand which advertising campaigns or website pages generated enquiries, when referral details are available.
  • To meet any applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

Contact details submitted in the booking form are used for the relevant service request. There is no payment collection through this website form.

4. How enquiries are stored and processed

The website sends booking information to a Google Apps Script used by TodayService. Service request details are stored in a business Google Sheets spreadsheet and sent to our business email using Google services. These providers may process information under their own applicable terms and safeguards.

We use the supplied details to handle your enquiry and related service communication. Data may also be made available to the TodayService technicians or staff directly involved in providing the requested service.

5. How long information is kept

Service enquiry records are kept for as long as reasonably needed to manage the repair request, customer follow-up, business records and legal obligations. There is no promise of automatic deletion after a fixed number of days.

You may ask us about your records or request deletion using the contact information below. We will review the request, subject to any necessary retention obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you previously submitted, or to raise concerns about its use. To help locate the request, please share the contact number or other details originally used. Avoid emailing sensitive information unnecessarily.

Email: todayservice942@gmail.com.

7. Advertising links and external services

Our website may receive visitors from Google Ads and other advertising links. URL referral parameters may be recorded with an enquiry. Phone and WhatsApp links open external calling or messaging applications governed by their respective services.

Third-party browser and hosting tools may use essential network data to display the website. This policy should be reviewed if new analytics, advertising tags, payment services or other integrations are added.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy as our processes or legal requirements change. An updated version will appear here with a revised date. For questions, contact TodayService.
