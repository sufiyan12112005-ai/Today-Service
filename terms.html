1. About the service

TodayService is an independent washing machine repair business providing inspections and suitable repairs through its own technicians in supported areas of Bengaluru, Karnataka. This website is intended for front-load and top-load washing machine service enquiries. Semi-automatic repairs are not offered through the booking form.

2. Website requests are not confirmed appointments

Submitting a website form or sending a WhatsApp message creates a request for our team to review. It does not automatically establish a confirmed visit time or guarantee that a technician will be available. A service arrangement is confirmed only after TodayService contacts you and the details are agreed.

3. Coverage and repair feasibility

Technician availability and coverage depend on your exact Bengaluru locality, appliance type and schedule. Specific faults, models and replacement parts may be outside our current capabilities. We will discuss the next step where we can.

4. Inspection, estimates and repair approval

Any applicable visit or inspection fee should be explained before a paid visit is arranged. Following diagnosis, our technician should discuss the proposed work, replacement parts where applicable and estimated repair charges. Repair work proceeds after the customer approves it.

Prices can vary by machine type, model, issue, parts and location. The website does not display a single fixed fee for all repairs.

5. Payment and warranty

No payment is collected through the online booking form. Payment arrangements are communicated during the actual service process. Any warranty that applies to specific repair work or replacement parts will be explained for that job; we do not advertise a blanket manufacturer warranty.

6. Cancellations and rescheduling

If you need to change or cancel a request, contact TodayService as early as possible at 9148104704 or todayservice942@gmail.com. Any charges already agreed for a technician visit will be handled according to those communicated terms and applicable law.

7. Independent business and appliance brands

TodayService is not a manufacturer's official service centre. Appliance brand names used on this site help customers describe their own machines, and do not indicate partnership, authorisation, sponsorship or endorsement by those manufacturers.

8. Customer safety and information

Customers should provide accurate contact and appliance information, and avoid operating equipment with obvious electrical or water-leak hazards. Do not dismantle or bypass safety equipment. A technician may decline unsafe work.
