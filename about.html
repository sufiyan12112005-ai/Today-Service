Who we are

TodayService is the business name used for our independent washing machine repair service in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Our own technicians carry out the inspection and repair work.

Customers can contact us directly through our website, telephone or WhatsApp. We review the reported issue, check the service locality and discuss available visit arrangements before a technician attends.

What our team repairs

We focus on front-load washing machines and top-load washing machines. Common requests include drainage problems, spin-cycle issues, leaks, unusual sounds, water filling concerns, power faults, door or lid issues and appliance error codes.

Whether a particular fault can be repaired depends on the model, inspection findings and availability of suitable replacement parts. We do not offer semi-automatic washing machine repair through this website.

How service is arranged

  1. Tell us the problem. Submit a request with the washing machine type, brand, phone number and Bengaluru PIN code, or call us directly.
  2. We confirm the next step. Our team contacts you to discuss the symptoms, technician coverage, visit timing and any applicable charges.
  3. Our technician inspects the machine. The fault, practical repair options, parts requirement and expected cost are discussed before repair work is approved.

Submitting the form does not automatically reserve an appointment slot. A visit is arranged after our team confirms details with you. Read our charges and service process.

Our independence from appliance manufacturers

TodayService is an independent repair service. We may repair models manufactured by LG, Samsung, IFB, Whirlpool, Bosch, Haier, Godrej and other brands, subject to model and service feasibility. Mentioning these companies helps customers identify the appliance they own.

We do not claim to be operated by, endorsed by, or authorised by these manufacturers unless a documented relationship is expressly identified. We do not use manufacturer logos as our business identity.
