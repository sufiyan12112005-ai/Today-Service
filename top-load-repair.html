Common top-load washer faults

Washer is not spinningThe drum or spin cycle may not start or finish as expected.
Lid does not lock or operate correctlyA lid or switch fault may interrupt a wash programme.
Machine does not fill or drainWater supply, inlet or drainage issues can prevent normal operation.
Excess vibration or unusual soundNoise or movement during a cycle may need inspection, especially if it suddenly becomes worse.
Washer will not startPower, control or safety-switch problems can produce similar symptoms.

How we handle service requests

Models and brands

We consider repair requests for top-load models from LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, IFB, Haier, Godrej and other brands, subject to local coverage, model and parts availability.

TodayService is not an official service centre for the named brands. Brand names are used to describe the washing machines customers own.

Before you call

  • Have the machine's brand and model ready if possible.
  • Describe when the problem occurs: washing, draining or spinning.
  • If water is leaking or you smell burning, stop using the machine and disconnect power if it is safe.
